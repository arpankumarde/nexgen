# NexGen

> **Where Data Meets Vision**

NexGen is the premier Data Science Community of Meghnad Saha Institute of Technology (MSIT). We are a student-driven platform dedicated to exploring the vast landscapes of Data Science, Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence.

---

## Our Vision

We believe data is the oil of the 21st century. Our mission is to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world impact through collaborative learning, innovation, and hands-on experimentation.

## Core Focus Areas

- **Deep Learning** — Exploring neural networks and advanced AI architectures.
- **Data Engineering** — Building robust pipelines and distributed systems.
- **MLOps** — Productionizing machine learning models with automated workflows.
- **Open Source** — Contributing to and building cutting-edge tools for the community.

## What We Do

- **Workshops** — Hands-on sessions on modern data tools and analytics.
- **Expert Talks** — Insights from industry leaders and tech innovators.
- **Hackathons** — Solving real-world problems through competitive coding.
- **Projects** — Collaborative research and development in emerging technologies.

## Community at a Glance

- **250+** Active Members
- **10+** Events Hosted
- **3+** Expert Sessions
